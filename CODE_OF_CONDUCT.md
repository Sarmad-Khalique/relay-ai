# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in ProvenWay a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age,
body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience,
education, socioeconomic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual
identity and orientation.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive, and
healthy community.

## Expected behavior

Be respectful, give and accept constructive feedback, focus on what is best for the community, show
empathy, and take responsibility for mistakes. Harassment, insults, threats, unwanted sexual attention,
publishing private information, and other conduct inappropriate in a professional setting are unacceptable.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove, edit, or reject contributions and temporarily or permanently ban
participants for behavior they deem inappropriate, threatening, offensive, or harmful. Report conduct
issues privately to the repository owner. All maintainers must respect the reporter's privacy and safety.

This policy is adapted from Contributor Covenant 2.1: https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/
